how to read this

  • every thor order lives on one shared order-number line, whatever the model. ayn ships each model in sweeps along that line, and publishes ranges like 2502xx with the last digits masked — so everything here works in blocks of ~100 orders.
  • each bar shows a model's frontier: the highest order number it has shipped past so far. the dashed line is your order — when your model's bar reaches it, your unit ships.
  • bars differ because models get swept at different times, not in fairness order. a model sitting behind yours isn't "late" — its sweep just hasn't come round yet. by the same token, the leader passing your number means nothing until your model's sweep arrives.
  • the eta extrapolates your model's shipping pace: the early end of the window assumes the recent working pace keeps up, the late end uses the long-run average including pauses. ayn has paused the whole line before (three weeks in july) — expect the window, not the headline date.
  • the step chart shows your model's frontier over time; flat stretches are pauses.
  • all of it is derived from ayn's public dashboard by extrapolation — an educated guess, not a promise, and not ayn's word.
